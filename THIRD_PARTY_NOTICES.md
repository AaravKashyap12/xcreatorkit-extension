## Screenshot editor artwork

The four painted skies are crops of nineteenth-century *Cloud Study* works in the Cleveland Museum of Art’s CC0 Open Access collection. The gallery names identify the crops.

- Peach Haze — [1980.112](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1980.112)
- Rain Wash — [1980.109](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1980.109)
- Silver Lining — [1980.113](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1980.113)
- Coral Evening — [1980.115](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1980.115)

Daylight Blue, Indigo Moon and Rose Hour use panoramas 04, 12 and 17 from [Cloudy Skyboxes by Screaming Brain Studios](https://opengameart.org/content/cloudy-skyboxes-0). The artist released the panoramas under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The extension includes local WebP copies.
