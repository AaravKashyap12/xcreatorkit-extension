# X Creator Kit extension privacy policy

This policy covers extension version 0.4.0. If your installed version is 0.3.0, see the [0.3.0 policy, including optional reply drafting](https://xcreatorkit.com/privacy.html).

## Screenshot editor

When you paste, drop or choose a screenshot, Chrome opens it locally in the editor. The image, an uploaded background, annotations, filename and any address you enter for a decorative browser frame stay in that editor’s memory. Closing or reloading the editor clears the open document. Choosing **Save this look** stores the style name and appearance settings in the browser. You can delete individual styles in **My styles**.

**Copy** writes the finished image to your clipboard. **Export** saves a file in the format you choose. Solid redactions cover the underlying pixels in that exported image. You control where you paste or share it and when to delete downloaded files.

## X readings and post images

Choosing **Read current X tab** on an Analytics, Rewards or profile page reads the matching figures already loaded in that tab. The extension saves the readings, associated account handle, UTC dates and settings in your local Chrome profile. The kit uses them to compare dates, track Rewards pace and create charts, cards and receipts.

The optional Following highlighter checks visible badges on your own Following page to tint eligible rows. The optional **Make an image of this post** action reads the public post you select. Chrome can take a temporary capture of the visible tab to crop the author’s photo locally. The editor discards the rest of that capture and uses initials when the photo is unavailable. Temporary selected-post data uses browser session storage.

## Sharing, permissions and deletion

X Creator Kit has no backend or telemetry. Version 0.4.0 removes reply drafting and the Gemini connection. It does not transmit, sell or share your readings, screenshots or other user data with the developer, advertisers or model providers. It does not read passwords, authentication tokens, cookies or direct messages. Pages you visit on X and external links you choose to open make their own requests. Sharing opens the destination you choose, and you decide whether to post.

You can revoke optional access to x.com in Chrome’s extension settings. Removing the extension clears its browser storage. Files already saved to your device and images already copied or shared are under your control. An update from 0.3.0 removes the retired reply settings and saved voice information.

The extension uses data available through Chrome's APIs only for its user-facing features and follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data). For privacy questions, contact [Aarav, the maintainer](https://x.com/byaarav).
